Project Origins & CTO

$RIZZ was launched on May 30, 2024 on Solana with a fixed supply of 1 billion tokens. It had no presale, no VC backing, no team wallets, and no vesting or emissions. The token was initially abandoned by its deployer.

In the weeks that followed, $RIZZ was adopted and revived by a decentralized online community through a Community Takeover (CTO). Since then, all development, liquidity, and infrastructure have been managed by community members.

Project Ethos

$RIZZ is not a utility or governance token. It is a meme-native, culturally embedded digital asset designed to mutate through Gen Alpha internet culture. The token references layers of ironic meme language — Sigma grindset, Skibidi-core, Fanum tax, and Ohio-core — and is driven by creative momentum from the Jungle Bay Artists Collective (aka the Galaxy Gas Interns).

There is no roadmap or centralized development agenda. All creative work and memetic activations are decentralized, spontaneous, and community-led.

Chain Structure

Solana: Native deployment. All 1B tokens were minted at genesis.

Base: Bridged via Wormhole. A portion of tokens have been ported to Base to increase accessibility and liquidity.

The Base contract uses standard proxy architecture. However, no upgrade permissions or mint functions are controlled by any active party.
